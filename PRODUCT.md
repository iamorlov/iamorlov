# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, collaborators, and fellow developers landing on a personal domain. Context: a 10-second first impression on desktop or phone. Job to be done: instantly know who Vadym Orlov is, what he does, and how to reach him.

## Product Purpose

Single-screen personal identity card for Vadym Orlov: name, one line of positioning ("Software Engineer and Game Developer"), three contact routes (GitHub, LinkedIn, Email). Success = the visitor remembers the name and clicks a link.

## Brand Personality

Split-screen duality: two panels, two tones, one person (engineer / game developer). Warm, confident, a little playful. Rounded type, matte flat color panels, living particle field.

## Locked identity (survived a full redesign-and-revert cycle; do not replace)

- The diagonal two-panel split: navy panel with cream VADYM, warm cream panel with navy ORLOV. Desktop splits left/right, mobile splits top/bottom.
- Directional text animation: VADYM enters from above, ORLOV from below.
- The mouse-reactive particle constellation background.
- Circular icon chips with labels for the three links.
- Gabarito as the typeface (chosen by the user; DM Sans, Outfit, Archivo, Sora all rejected).

## Anti-references

- Dark moody portfolio templates (a full matte-dark redesign was built and reverted; the user wants the bright split identity).
- Font weight 900 (too heavy), outline/hollow display text (renders broken with negative tracking).
- Glossy neon, crypto gradients, glassmorphism, gradient text.

## Design Principles

- Refine, never replace: polish the existing identity instead of inventing a new one.
- One screen, one idea: everything visible without scrolling on every device.
- Content is frozen: name, one positioning line, three links. Never invent copy.
- Motion as craft: directional entrance choreography plus quiet idle life; expo-out curves, no springs/bounce.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast on all text. Full keyboard path with visible focus. `prefers-reduced-motion` collapses choreography to fades and stills the particle field. Touch targets ≥44px. Split-letter animation hidden from screen readers behind accessible labels.
