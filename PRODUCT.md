# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, collaborators, and fellow developers landing on a personal domain. Context: a 10-second first impression on desktop or phone. Job to be done: instantly know who Vadym Orlov is, what he does, and how to reach him.

## Product Purpose

Single-screen personal identity card for Vadym Orlov. Not a CV, not a project gallery: a name, one line of positioning ("Software Engineer and Game Developer."), and three contact routes (GitHub, LinkedIn, Email). Success = the visitor remembers the name and clicks a link.

## Brand Personality

Machined, nocturnal, precise. The site should feel like a well-made physical object in low light: matte surfaces, one sapphire edge. Confidence through restraint and finish quality, not through volume of content.

## Anti-references

- Glossy neon / crypto-gradient dark sites (purple-cyan glows, glassmorphism).
- Terminal/hacker costume (green mono text, scanlines) despite the engineering subject.
- Generic Vercel-black clone: pure #000, Inter, centered stack.
- Template portfolio: avatar circle, skill badges, card grids.

## Design Principles

- One screen, one idea: everything visible without scrolling on every device.
- Typography IS the imagery: the name carries the visual weight; no stock photos, no illustrations.
- Matte over gloss: grain, soft light, low-chroma cool darks; a single sapphire accent used sparingly.
- Motion as craft signature: one orchestrated load choreography plus quiet idle life; never decorative confetti.
- Content is frozen: name, one positioning line, three links. Never invent copy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast on all text. Full keyboard path with visible focus. `prefers-reduced-motion` collapses choreography to simple fades. Touch targets ≥44px on mobile. Decorative split-letter animation hidden from screen readers behind accessible labels.
